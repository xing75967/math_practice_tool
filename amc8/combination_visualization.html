🍔 Food Combo Connection Machine

🎯 Big Math Idea

Each Main Dish can connect to MANY drinks. Every connection creates a NEW two-digit number.

The left side is the TENS digit. The middle side is the ONES digit.

3 × 4 = 12 combinations

🍔 Tens Digit

🍔 Burger = 1
🍕 Pizza = 2
🌭 Hot Dog = 3

🥤 Ones Digit

🥤 Cola = 1
🧃 Juice = 2
🥛 Milk = 3
🍵 Tea = 4

🧠 What Students Should Notice

  • One tens digit can connect to MANY ones digits.
  • Every new connection creates a NEW number.
  • 1 makes: 11, 12, 13, 14
  • 2 makes: 21, 22, 23, 24
  • 3 makes: 31, 32, 33, 34
  • So we get 12 total combinations.
