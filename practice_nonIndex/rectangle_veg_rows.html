Fish Pond & Vegetable Farm

Understanding Area Using Geometry Visualization

1. Square Pond

The field height is 40m.

The pond is a square using the full height.

So the pond is:

40m × 40m

2. Remaining Farm

Total field length: 90m

Pond used: 40m

Remaining:

90 − 40 = 50m

3. Farm Area

Vegetable farm:

40 × 50

Farm area:

2000m²

4. Vegetable Rows

1 vegetable row for every 20m²

Number of rows:

2000 ÷ 20

= 100 rows

Understanding the Grid
The farm is divided into small 1m² squares.

Total farm area:

2000m²

Each vegetable row needs:

20m²

So:

2000 ÷ 20 = 100

Final Answer

100
vegetable rows

Original Problem

A farmer owns a rectangular field measuring 40m × 90m.

A square fish pond is built on one side of the field, and the remaining area is used as a vegetable farm.

If the farmer plants 1 vegetable row for every 20 square meters of farm area, how many vegetable rows can he plant?

Answer: 100 vegetable rows
